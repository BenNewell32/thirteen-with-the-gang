How to play
  • Everyone gets 13 cards. Get rid of yours first.
  • Cards run 3 (low) up to 2 (high). Suits break ties: ♠ ♣ ♦ ♥, low to high.
  • Play a single, pair, triple, or a run of 3 or more in a row (no 2s in runs).
  • Beat the pile with the same kind of play, but higher. Or pass. Once you pass you sit out until the pile clears.
  • Bombs: four of a kind or three pairs in a row beats a single 2. Four pairs in a row beats a pair of 2s.
  • A random player starts the first game. After that, the winner leads the next one.
